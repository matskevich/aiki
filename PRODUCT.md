# aiki — your personal agi

a modular agent harness for your own life.

the product helps an existing agent turn a person's intention into a useful,
verified result across their own accounts and devices. context, skills, tools,
durable work and feedback form one system. personal agi is a direction, not a
claim that this collection has achieved general intelligence.

## philosophy

carry accepted work through its outcome. recover known facts and prior authority
before asking a person to repeat them. act on available authorized work and make
a genuinely missing decision easy to answer. treat attention and recovery effort
as real costs. preserve provider controls, budget scope and unknown effects.

learn from actual outcomes rather than accumulating longer prompts. a technical
fix, a contextual recipe, a general principle and a personal preference are
different results. portable rules should transfer across people while leaving
their interaction preferences and risk choices configurable.

## one source, two delivery channels

the private owning repositories are the development source. `aiki-preview` is a
closed read-and-feedback distribution for admitted friends. public `aiki` receives
selected stable modules and examples. the personal overlay configures behavior
and authority without maintaining another code fork.

each provider keeps its source, account identities and release gates. the private
`agent-connectors` project owns this generic product subtree and common runtimes.
raw correspondence, personal models, sessions and resolved secrets are not
distribution material. installing code gives no access to the maintainer's life.

## modules and levels

use the functional, product, usability, technical and infrastructure views in
`docs/views/`. the catalog describes providers, devices, task coordination,
observations, conversation, skills, attention, projections and domain consumers.
the available operation and its exact schema always come from the actual provider.

portable skills in this delivery cover outcome continuity, attention and authority,
and learning from reality. they do not replace personal domain context or claim
that every linked private runtime is already packaged for independent adoption.

## accepted product evidence

useful result, effort required from the person, continuation without duplicate
effects, and cost of setup/update/recovery. distinguish source checks, packaging,
actual audience access and observed use. keep unknowns visible in `CURRENT.md`.
