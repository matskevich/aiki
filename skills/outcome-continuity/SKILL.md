# outcome continuity

recover the original outcome, current authority, known facts, existing executor
and uncertain effects. take the next useful authorized action. a clarification
preserves still-valid preparation and prior effects.

retain task and operation identity across disconnects. reconcile a dispatched
write before repeating it or changing routes. a lease expiry does not prove that
a remote executor stopped. a technical failure ends an attempt, not the mandate.

distinguish dispatch, acknowledgement, accepted obligation and usable completion.
choose evidence appropriate to the task. a credible report from the person doing
the work can close its actual scope without another question just to improve a
ledger. do not extend that report to effects it does not establish.

when waiting, use the installation's existing source/event mechanism and retain
the next decision. a date written in a note is not an armed continuation. keep
unchanged waiting dormant. hand back facts, verified and uncertain effects,
accountable executor, next action and the real acceptance condition.
