---
name: reality-learning
description: Turn a real operational episode or correction into a tested technical improvement, contextual recipe, transferable principle or personal preference. Use after a meaningful outcome or failure, not as an audit of every routine message.
---

# learning from reality

recover the intended outcome, observed result and minimum source evidence. fix
current harm within authority before expanding the explanation. separate source
observation, mechanism hypothesis, experiment and actual effect.

classify the lesson. a reproducible defect belongs in technical code or a small
skill. a condition-dependent workflow belongs in a recipe. a general principle
needs a mechanism and a counterexample beyond one person's case. an individual's
interaction preference stays in their private profile.

one episode need not create a new rule. test whether a simpler model, existing
rule applied correctly, focused bug fix or known limitation explains the outcome.
choose a bounded reversible improvement and observe the next comparable case.
source change, delivery, loaded instruction and useful outcome are separate facts.

raw correspondence and personal evidence remain with their authorized owner.
shared cases contain a sanitized mechanism, limits and synthetic reproduction.
code access does not authorize sharing another person's data or operating their
accounts. retain private provenance outside exported artifacts and use the
installation's existing learning ingress rather than creating another model loop.

if private material arrives in a feedback surface, stop reproducing it and check
its actual visibility. use the installation's authorized restriction or redaction
route when available. do not promise deletion or wider sharing without authority
and readback of the real effect.
